## 2. Introductory First Comment (Post immediately after submission)

```markdown
Hi HN! I built FreelanceToolkit because I was tired of paying $30/month for basic invoicing software that held my data hostage and required a subscription just to spit out a PDF invoice.

Most invoicing and freelance rate calculators on the web today are lead magnets disguised as software: they demand your email, force you into a 14-day trial, and send your clients' financial details to a third-party server.

FreelanceToolkit is completely different:
- **100% Client-Side:** Everything runs in your local browser sandbox. No server, no remote database, no tracking pixels.
- **Privacy First:** Your client names, rates, and invoice history stay strictly in `localStorage` or are printed directly to PDF.
- **Print-Native Engine:** Uses CSS `@media print` rules and native browser vector rendering to produce crisp, professional invoices without heavy server-side PDF renderers or canvas hacks.
- **Multi-Currency Pricing Math:** Includes a rate calculator that factors in unbillable overhead (admin, proposals, taxes) across USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, and AUD so independent workers don't undercharge.
- **Zero Dependencies:** Pure vanilla JavaScript and utility CSS, hosted on GitHub Pages.

Live App: https://komasan19283645.github.io/freelance-toolkit/index-en.html
GitHub Repo: https://github.com/komasan19283645/freelance-toolkit

I'd love feedback from the HN community on the CSS print rules, mobile layout edge cases, or features you'd like to see in a zero-dependency static tool!
```
