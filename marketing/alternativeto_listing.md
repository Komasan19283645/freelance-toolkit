## 3. Short & Long Descriptions

### Short Description (under 250 characters):
A lightweight, 100% private and client-side invoice generator and hourly rate calculator for freelancers and small businesses. Zero signup, zero tracking, works directly in the browser.

### Long Description:
FreelanceToolkit is a privacy-first, zero-dependency suite of client-side tools designed for freelancers, independent contractors, and solo entrepreneurs who don't want to pay monthly SaaS fees just to issue invoices.

Unlike typical cloud-based accounting platforms that require email signups and store your billing records on remote servers, FreelanceToolkit runs 100% locally in your browser. All invoice details, client names, and tax calculations are handled strictly via client-side JavaScript and browser-native print stylesheets.

### Key Features:
- **Zero Registration Required:** Generate and download professional PDF invoices immediately.
- **Multi-Currency Support:** Seamlessly compute subtotals and taxes in USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), CAD (CA$), and AUD (A$).
- **Client-Side Privacy:** No telemetry, no remote databases, no analytics trackers.
- **Freelance Rate Calculator:** Built-in hourly rate math taking into account unbillable hours (marketing, proposals, admin), operational overhead, and tax buffers.
- **Print-to-PDF Formatting:** Uses native browser CSS print media for crisp, vector-sharp PDF export without watermarks.
